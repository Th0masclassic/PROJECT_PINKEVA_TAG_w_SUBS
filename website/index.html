GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Do I need a subscription for the card to work?+

No. The card’s selected finder identity continues advertising without a subscription. PINKEVA Cloud+ is for the services marked *.

Does renewal need a Bluetooth connection?+

No. Subscription renewal updates cloud access only. Nothing new is sent to the card and you do not need to have it nearby.

Will it show a live GPS position?+

No. PINKEVA is a Bluetooth crowd-network tracker, not a continuous GPS device. The app shows the latest available report and its timestamp.

Does the card work with iPhone and Android?+

Yes. PINKEVA is designed for iPhone and Android, with support for the Apple Find My network and Google Find Hub. During setup, the app selects the network that matches your phone; the card uses one finding network at a time.

Can I make the card ring?+

Yes. Play Sound lets you ring the card from the PINKEVA app when it is within Bluetooth range. Final sound level and range depend on the production ring hardware.
