Questions before
you carry on?

We’d rather be clear about how PINQEVA works than hide the important bits in a footer.

Get your PINQEVA Card
Do I need a subscription for the card to work?

No. The card’s selected finder identity continues advertising without a subscription. PINQEVA Cloud is required for cloud location retrieval, history, alerts, safe zones, recovery tools, and vehicle protection.

Does renewal need a Bluetooth connection?

No. Renewal updates your cloud access on the backend. Nothing has to be written to the physical card, so you don’t need to find it, connect to it, or send it a new expiry date.

Can I use PINQEVA with iPhone and Android?

PINQEVA is being built with both platform paths in mind. You select the matching setup during provisioning. Final Apple Find My and Google Find Hub operation remains subject to each platform’s certification and partner requirements.

Will the map show a live GPS position?

PINQEVA is a Bluetooth crowd-network tracker, not a continuous GPS device. The app shows the latest available network report, its timestamp, and the history your subscription retains.

What can I put the card in?

It is designed for card slots, wallets, bags, travel cases, and a vehicle. Anything you would rather find than replace is a good place to start.
