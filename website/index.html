GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Do I need a subscription for the card to work?+

No. The card’s selected finder identity continues advertising without a subscription. PINQEVA Cloud is for the services marked *.

Does renewal need a Bluetooth connection?+

No. Subscription renewal updates cloud access only. Nothing new is sent to the card and you do not need to have it nearby.

Will it show a live GPS position?+

No. PINQEVA is a Bluetooth crowd-network tracker, not a continuous GPS device. The app shows the latest available report and its timestamp.

Can I set up iPhone and Android?+

Choose the appropriate path during setup. Finder-network availability remains subject to platform requirements, approval, and regional rollout.
