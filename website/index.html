GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
made clear.

Is this a live GPS tracker?

No. PINKEVA is a Bluetooth crowd-network tracker. Its location is the latest available report, with a timestamp, rather than a continuous live GPS position. Coverage and reporting intervals vary.

Do I need Cloud+ for every card?

One active Cloud+ account subscription covers all cards you own. It unlocks services such as latest reports, history, alerts and recovery tools. The card’s finder broadcasts continue without a subscription; network access remains subject to platform requirements.

Does it support iPhone and Android?

The same card is designed to receive both Apple Find My and Google Find Hub identities during setup. This preview does not establish platform approval or certification. Confirm current compatibility and regional availability before purchase.

Can I buy a card now?

Online card sales are not open yet. You can review a selection here, but no order, reservation or payment is created.

Is the 3D view the inside of the card?

No. It illustrates the relationship between the card, Bluetooth finding and the app using the existing product artwork. It is not a hardware teardown or a technical drawing.
